# Submission evidence gates

KDD is provisional. These gates organize the research; they do not guarantee
acceptance or certify CCF A quality.

| Gate | Present evidence | Remaining work |
|---|---|---|
| Reproduction | Six-trial metrics/checkpoints reconciled; negative combined comparison | Extend markets and from-scratch expert training |
| Data identity | Local source files and audits | Complete bytes archive, remote recovery, upstream feature-causality audit |
| Originality | Cost-transport prototype | Distinct mechanism and current primary-source novelty review |
| Effectiveness | Three markets, 84 validation settings | Paired seeds, comparable baselines and stable gains |
| Confounds | Activity-matched diagnostic | Matched turnover/risk/exposure controls for the next method |
| Generalization | No held-out gain claimed | Freeze hypotheses, protect test periods, cross-market tests |
| Execution | Original assumptions documented | Next-bar fills and spread/slippage/delay/impact stress where supported |
| Paper | Evidence/workflow records | Complete manuscript, supplement and independent review |

Ten datasets and ten methods including two from 2026 remain planning targets,
not completed results. Relevance and fair comparisons take priority over counts.

## Next candidate question — unproven

Can memory reuse be gated by **matured prediction errors** for each expert and
action when feedback is delayed and the market changes? Gates may update only
when an outcome becomes available. Compare against fixed rejection, recency
weighting, simple online expert weighting, equal-activity random controls and
no-memory ablations. Separate cost/reward changes from transition changes.

Novelty and effectiveness are unproven. Generic successor features, uncertainty
penalties or fewer trades are insufficient contributions. Freeze the next
mechanism and selection protocol before evaluating the reserved test periods.
