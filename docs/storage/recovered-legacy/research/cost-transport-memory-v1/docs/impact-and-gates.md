# Route impact and research completion gates

Old paper PDFs, data provenance, source patches and completed-trial artifacts remain reusable for historical reproduction. The original ETH test stays historical and cannot choose new methods. Final remote metrics are currently unavailable because both known SSH entry points time out. The last cached progress from October 2 is not current evidence.

Reuse: original implementation audit, meaningful equivalence tests, failure logs, validation-frozen candidate, resource ownership conventions.
Needs revalidation: final three-seed metrics, expert provenance, original feature causality, cross-machine reproducibility, realistic fills, all new literature baseline adaptations.
Not transferable: claiming the published 39.28% under a new denominator, treating March 2026 pilot results as the original ETH benchmark, or treating hand-coded controls as ten published baselines.

| Gate | Required evidence | Current status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reproduction | Six sealed-test artifacts, verified checkpoints and immutable configs | Remote unavailable; unknown |
| Novelty | Clear distinction from MacroHFT, SF/GPI, RaSF, RSF and 2026 trading work | Generic cost transport fails this gate; research question remains provisional |
| Causality | Prefix-invariance tests; chronological reward maturity; train-only preprocessing | Six diagnostic tests pass; original feature generator still unaudited |
| Mechanism | Discriminating ablations beyond low turnover/cash fallback | Three-asset validation-only component pilot |
| Coverage | Ten relevant markets and ten compatible recent methods, including two 2026 methods | Planning target; three pilot markets, zero fully reproduced new literature baselines |
| Reliability | At least five paired training seeds where stochastic; calendar-block uncertainty; adverse regime breakdowns | Pending, not replaceable with iid minute bootstrap |
| Execution | Next-observation fills, costs, latency, capacity/impact stress and transaction traces | Pilot next-bar linear-fee only; impact/data calibration pending |
| Generalization | Predeclared unseen periods/assets; no test-based search | July–September 2026 reserved, not downloaded |
| Review | Correctness, statistics, novelty, reproducibility and venue reviews; verified fixes | Not yet at pre-submission review stage |
| Manuscript | Evidence-linked complete English paper, sources, figures, compiled/rendered PDF | Not written as a finished paper because scientific evidence is insufficient |

Do not advance a route merely because its best reported number increases. If the guard yields cash-like behavior or cross-asset degradation, preserve that negative result and identify the next discriminating experiment. No claim of CCF A readiness is justified until the gates are resolved. This is not a guarantee of acceptance.
